A screenshare app for small engineering syncs, where shared windows stay real windows.

[Install Petal &rarr;](/docs/getting-started/install/)

## What is Petal

Petal is a low-latency screenshare app for macOS and Windows, built for small engineering
syncs — typically 3 to 8 people. When you share a window, it doesn't show up
on other participants' screens as a video tile in a grid. It arrives as a
real, independently movable, borderless native window on their own desktop —
one they can drag, resize, and arrange alongside their other windows, just
like anything else running on their machine.

## Who it's for

Petal is built for small engineering teams: pairing sessions,
code review, debugging together, design reviews — the kind of sync where more
than one shared window matters and where the overhead of a traditional
video-call grid gets in the way.

## Why this is different

Most screenshare tools capture a window and re-render it as a video feed
inside their own UI — a tile you can't move independently of the call
window, can't overlap with your other work the way you'd want, and that adds
a layer of app chrome between you and the content. Petal's compositor instead
gives each shared window its own native window on the receiving end. You can
move it, resize it, put it behind or in front of your other windows — because
it behaves like one of your own windows, not like a video embedded in someone
else's app.

## Requirements

Petal runs on macOS 13 or later, with one universal build for Apple Silicon
and Intel Macs. A Windows x86-64 NSIS installer is also published. Windows
support is still early, so expect platform-specific rough edges. The current
Windows installer is not Authenticode-signed and may trigger a Microsoft
SmartScreen warning on first download; the Tauri updater signature still
protects automatic updates after installation.
