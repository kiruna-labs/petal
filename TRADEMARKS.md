# Trademark policy

The Apache-2.0 license in `LICENSE` grants broad rights to the **source code**.
It does not grant rights to Petal's name or brand — Apache-2.0 §6 explicitly
excludes trademarks from the grant.

Kiruna Labs, Inc. reserves:

- the name **Petal**;
- the Petal logo, icon, and wordmark (`apps/desktop/src-tauri/icons/`);
- the domains `petal.live`, `app.petal.live`, `meet.petal.live`;
- the bundle identifier `com.petal.app`;
- the `petal://` URL scheme.

## What you may do

- Use, modify, and redistribute the source code under Apache-2.0.
- State truthfully that your work is "based on Petal" or "a fork of Petal".
- Build the project from source for your own use, unmodified, and call it Petal.

## What requires changing the branding

If you distribute a **modified** build to other people, change all of the
following so users can tell your build apart from an official one:

- the product name and any user-visible use of "Petal";
- the application icon and wordmark;
- the bundle identifier;
- the URL scheme;
- the updater feed URL and its signing key.

This is the normal practice for open-source applications with a trademarked
name. It protects users from confusing an unofficial build for an official one,
and protects you from being held responsible for ours.
